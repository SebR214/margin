# People do not always pay the official rate for a dollar

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

Where banks ration dollars, people buy stablecoins from each other and pay more.

We compare what a dollar costs in 54 countries with the official rate every hour, and also price sending money on 9 routes with every fee we can measure.

AI agents built this site and keep it running. So far they have stored 535,288 prices from 41 sources over 1,558 hours.

# People do not always pay the official rate for a dollar

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 28 Oct.

Where banks ration dollars, people buy stablecoins from each other and pay more.

We compare what a dollar costs in 54 countries with the official rate every hour. On 11 routes we also check what it costs to send money there, by transfer app and by stablecoin, with every fee we can measure. Where a dollar costs more than the official rate, a stablecoin can be the cheaper way to send.

AI agents built this site and keep it running. So far they have stored 601,988 prices from 41 sources over 1,645 hours.

## When the official rate finally moved

In the history we hold, an official rate jumped 10% or more 5 times while a dollar already cost at least 10% more on exchanges. The bar shows how much of that gap each jump closed.

Argentina, December 2023: a dollar cost 151% more on exchanges than at the official rate. Three weeks after the jump, the official rate had closed 82% of that gap.

# People do not always pay the official rate for a dollar

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

Where banks ration dollars, people buy stablecoins from each other and pay more.

We compare what a dollar costs in 53 countries with the official rate every hour, and also price sending money on 7 routes with every fee we can measure.

AI agents built this site and keep it running. So far they have stored 502,529 prices from 36 sources over 1,507 hours.

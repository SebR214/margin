## 6 countries had no price at 15:00 UTC

Afghanistan, Angola, Mongolia, Mozambique, Syria, Uganda.

- Afghanistan: too few people selling to call it a price
- Angola: too few people selling to call it a price
- Mongolia: too few people selling to call it a price
- Mozambique: the buying and selling sides are not the same market here
- Syria: too few people selling to call it a price
- Uganda: the buying and selling sides are not the same market here

We also check Botswana, Brunei, Ethiopia, Ghana, Nepal every hour, but too few people sell dollars there to give a price.

Sudan has a price but is left off the scale. Its official rate is frozen, so the gap measures the freeze, not what people pay.

## 5 countries had no price at 04:00 UTC

Angola, Lebanon, Syria, Uganda, West African CFA.

- Lebanon: too few people selling to call it a price

Sudan has a price but is left off the scale. Its official rate is frozen, so the gap measures the freeze, not what people pay.

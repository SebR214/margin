## 4 countries had no price at 08:00 UTC

Mongolia, Uganda, West African CFA, South Africa.

- Mongolia: too few people selling to call it a price
- Uganda: Held back this hour: the price jumped from its usual level and we’re waiting for a second source to confirm it.
- West African CFA: the buying and selling sides are not the same market here
- South Africa: Held back this hour: the price jumped from its usual level and we’re waiting for a second source to confirm it.

We also check Afghanistan, Botswana, Brunei, Ethiopia, Ghana, Nepal every hour, but too few people sell dollars there to give a price.

Sudan has a price but is left off the scale. Its official rate is frozen, so the gap measures the freeze, not what people pay.

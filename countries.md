## 4 countries had no price at 05:00 UTC

Mongolia, Mozambique, Syria, South Africa.

- Mongolia: too few people selling to call it a price
- Mozambique: the buying and selling sides are not the same market here
- Syria: too few people selling to call it a price
- South Africa: too few people selling to call it a price

We also check Afghanistan, Botswana, Brunei, Ethiopia, Ghana, Nepal every hour, but too few people sell dollars there to give a price.

Sudan has a price but is left off the scale. Its official rate is frozen, so the gap measures the freeze, not what people pay.

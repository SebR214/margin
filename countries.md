## 6 countries had no price at 02:00 UTC

Angola, Bolivia, Egypt, Lebanon, Syria, West African CFA.

- Angola: too few people selling to call it a price
- Bolivia: Held back this hour: the price jumped from its usual level and we’re waiting for a second source to confirm it.
- Egypt: the buying and selling sides are not the same market here
- Lebanon: too few people selling to call it a price
- Syria: too few people selling to call it a price
- West African CFA: the buying and selling sides are not the same market here

We also check Afghanistan, Botswana, Brunei, Ethiopia, Ghana, Nepal every hour, but too few people sell dollars there to give a price.

Sudan has a price but is left off the scale. Its official rate is frozen, so the gap measures the freeze, not what people pay.

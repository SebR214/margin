# In Pakistan a dollar costs 2.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 277.01 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 283.97 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,397 PKR. The official rate says 27,701 PKR.

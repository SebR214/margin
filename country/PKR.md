# In Pakistan a dollar costs 3.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 276.73 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 285.74 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,574 PKR. The official rate says 27,673 PKR.

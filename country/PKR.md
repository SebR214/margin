# In Pakistan a dollar costs 3.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 276.94 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 285.55 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,555 PKR. The official rate says 27,694 PKR.

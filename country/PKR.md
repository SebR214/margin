# In Pakistan a dollar costs 2.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 276.99 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 284.57 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,457 PKR. The official rate says 27,699 PKR.

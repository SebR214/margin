# In Pakistan a dollar costs 2.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 277.01 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 284.70 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,470 PKR. The official rate says 27,701 PKR.

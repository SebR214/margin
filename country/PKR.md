# In Pakistan a dollar costs 3.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 276.81 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 285.57 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,557 PKR. The official rate says 27,681 PKR.

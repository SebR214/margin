# In Pakistan a dollar costs 3.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 276.82 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 285.32 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,532 PKR. The official rate says 27,682 PKR.

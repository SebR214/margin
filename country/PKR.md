# In Pakistan a dollar costs 2.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 277 PKR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 285.01 PKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 28,501 PKR. The official rate says 27,700 PKR.

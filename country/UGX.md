# In Uganda a dollar costs 6.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 4,126.43 UGX per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,390 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 438,983 UGX. The official rate says 412,643 UGX.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

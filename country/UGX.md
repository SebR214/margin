# In Uganda a dollar costs 7.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 4,113.32 UGX per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,417 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 441,700 UGX. The official rate says 411,332 UGX.

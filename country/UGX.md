# In Uganda a dollar costs 5.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 4,126.43 UGX per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,331 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 433,100 UGX. The official rate says 412,643 UGX.

# In Uganda a dollar costs 7.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 4,113.32 UGX per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,400 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 439,999 UGX. The official rate says 411,332 UGX.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Uganda a dollar costs 3.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 4,336.26 UGX per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,468 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 446,781 UGX. The official rate says 433,626 UGX.

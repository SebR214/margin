# In Uganda a dollar costs 5.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 4,040.74 UGX per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,252 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 425,172 UGX. The official rate says 404,074 UGX.

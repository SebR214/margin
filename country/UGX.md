# In Uganda a dollar costs 4.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 4,040.74 UGX per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,211 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

For US$100, people pay 421,117 UGX. The official rate says 404,074 UGX.

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

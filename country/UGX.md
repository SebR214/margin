# In Uganda a dollar costs 6.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 4,229.34 UGX per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,496 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 449,645 UGX. The official rate says 422,934 UGX.

# In Uganda a dollar costs 3.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 4,100.68 UGX per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,249 UGX. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 424,870 UGX. The official rate says 410,068 UGX.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

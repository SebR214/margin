# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 18,089.97 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,105 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,111 IDR, 0.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,810,500 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,997 IDR.

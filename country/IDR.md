# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 18,030.16 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,044 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,090 IDR, 0.3% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,804,400 IDR. The official rate says 1,803,016 IDR.

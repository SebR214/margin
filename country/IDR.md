# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 18,087 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,116 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,162 IDR, 0.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,811,600 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,700 IDR.

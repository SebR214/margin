# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.4% less than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 18,001.6 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 17,931 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 17,989 IDR, 0.1% under the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,793,100 IDR. The official rate says 1,800,160 IDR.

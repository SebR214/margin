# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 18,087 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,117 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,159 IDR, 0.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,811,650 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,700 IDR.

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.1% less than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 18,082.48 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,072 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,092 IDR, 0.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,807,200 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,248 IDR.

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct

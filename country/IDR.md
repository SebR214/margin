# In Indonesia a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 18,089.97 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,098 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,108 IDR, 0.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,809,750 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,997 IDR.

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

This price is set from order books. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

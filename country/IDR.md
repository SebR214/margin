# In Indonesia a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 18,082.48 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,079 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,099 IDR, 0.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,807,900 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,248 IDR.

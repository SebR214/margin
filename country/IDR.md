# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 18,090.29 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,100 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,157 IDR, 0.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,809,950 IDR. The official rate says 1,809,029 IDR.

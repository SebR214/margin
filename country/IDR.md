# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 18,090.29 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,117 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,154 IDR, 0.4% over the official rate.

# In Indonesia a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 18,082.48 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,077 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,157 IDR, 0.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,807,700 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,248 IDR.

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is typical of the record since 10 Aug 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between −0.6% and 0.0%.

On 6 Oct this number started coming from order books instead of person-to-person ads.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from order books. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

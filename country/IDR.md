# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 18,148.81 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,173 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,192 IDR, 0.2% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,817,250 IDR. The official rate says 1,814,881 IDR.

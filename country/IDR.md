# In Indonesia a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 18,030.16 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,075 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,177 IDR, 0.8% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,807,500 IDR. The official rate says 1,803,016 IDR.

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct

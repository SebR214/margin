# In Indonesia a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 18,087 IDR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Indonesia trade stablecoins, one costs 18,088 IDR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18,140 IDR, 0.3% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,808,750 IDR. The official rate says 1,808,700 IDR.

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

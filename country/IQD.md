# In Iraq a dollar costs 12.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 1,517.42 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,712 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 171,232 IQD. The official rate says 151,742 IQD.

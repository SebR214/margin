# In Iraq a dollar costs 13.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 1,517.24 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,718 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 171,802 IQD. The official rate says 151,724 IQD.

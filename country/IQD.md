# In Iraq a dollar costs 13.3% more than the official rate

The official rate is 1,517.24 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,720 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 171,966 IQD. The official rate says 151,724 IQD.

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

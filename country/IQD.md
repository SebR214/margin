# In Iraq a dollar costs 12.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 1,517.28 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,712 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 171,217 IQD. The official rate says 151,728 IQD.

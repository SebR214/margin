# In Iraq a dollar costs 13.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 1,517.53 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,719 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 171,946 IQD. The official rate says 151,753 IQD.

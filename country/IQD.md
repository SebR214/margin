# In Iraq a dollar costs 13.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 1,517.24 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,717 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 171,665 IQD. The official rate says 151,724 IQD.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

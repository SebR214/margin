Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 1,517.43 IQD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,732 IQD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

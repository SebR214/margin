# In Syria a dollar costs 19.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 121.75 SYP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 145.45 SYP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 14,545 SYP. The official rate says 12,175 SYP.

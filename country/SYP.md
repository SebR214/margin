# In Syria a dollar costs 18.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 121.81 SYP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 144.80 SYP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 14,479 SYP. The official rate says 12,181 SYP.

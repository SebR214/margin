# In Syria a dollar costs 18.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 121.91 SYP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 144.00 SYP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 14,400 SYP. The official rate says 12,191 SYP.

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

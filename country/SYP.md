# In Syria a dollar costs 18.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 121.82 SYP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 144.15 SYP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 14,415 SYP. The official rate says 12,182 SYP.

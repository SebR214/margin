# In Syria a dollar costs 18.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 121.9 SYP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 144.60 SYP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 14,460 SYP. The official rate says 12,190 SYP.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

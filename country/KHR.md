# In Cambodia a dollar costs 2.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 4,103.95 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,211 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 421,075 KHR. The official rate says 410,395 KHR.

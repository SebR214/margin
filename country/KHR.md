# In Cambodia a dollar costs 1.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 4,103.95 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,183 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 418,325 KHR. The official rate says 410,395 KHR.

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct

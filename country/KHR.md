# In Cambodia a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

The official rate is 4,052.84 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,136 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 413,575 KHR. The official rate says 405,284 KHR.

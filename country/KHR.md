# In Cambodia a dollar costs 1.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 4,063.92 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,114 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 411,425 KHR. The official rate says 406,392 KHR.

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$3,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

# In Cambodia a dollar costs 2.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 4,060.03 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,152 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 415,225 KHR. The official rate says 406,003 KHR.

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$4,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Cambodia a dollar costs 2.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 4,059.85 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,158 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 415,750 KHR. The official rate says 405,985 KHR.

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

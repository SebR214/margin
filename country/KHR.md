# In Cambodia a dollar costs 3.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 4,060.03 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,209 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 420,875 KHR. The official rate says 406,003 KHR.

# In Cambodia a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 4,105 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,188 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 418,750 KHR. The official rate says 410,500 KHR.

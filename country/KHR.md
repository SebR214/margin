# In Cambodia a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 4,063.92 KHR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 4,144 KHR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 414,375 KHR. The official rate says 406,392 KHR.

# In Central African CFA a dollar costs 6.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 584.82 XAF per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 624.83 XAF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 62,483 XAF. The official rate says 58,482 XAF.

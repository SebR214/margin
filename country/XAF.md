# In Central African CFA a dollar costs 6.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 585.24 XAF per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 624.08 XAF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 62,408 XAF. The official rate says 58,524 XAF.

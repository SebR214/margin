# In Central African CFA a dollar costs 6.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 585.24 XAF per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 623.44 XAF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

For US$100, people pay 62,344 XAF. The official rate says 58,524 XAF.

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

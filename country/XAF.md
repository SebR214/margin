# In Central African CFA a dollar costs 6.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 585.53 XAF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 624.40 XAF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 62,440 XAF. The official rate says 58,553 XAF.

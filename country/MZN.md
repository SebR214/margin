# In Mozambique a dollar costs 17.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 63.7 MZN per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 74.98 MZN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 7,497 MZN. The official rate says 6,370 MZN.

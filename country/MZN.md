# In Mozambique a dollar costs 17.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 63.72 MZN per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 74.89 MZN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 7,489 MZN. The official rate says 6,372 MZN.

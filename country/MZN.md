# In Mozambique a dollar costs 17.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 63.65 MZN per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 74.70 MZN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 7,469 MZN. The official rate says 6,365 MZN.

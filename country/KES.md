# In Kenya a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 129.87 KES per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 129.84 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 12,984 KES. The official rate says 12,987 KES.

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 129.87 KES per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 130.43 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 13,043 KES. The official rate says 12,987 KES.

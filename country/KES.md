# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 129.81 KES per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 130.11 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 13,011 KES. The official rate says 12,981 KES.

# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 129.84 KES per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 131.06 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 13,106 KES. The official rate says 12,984 KES.

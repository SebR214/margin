# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 129.84 KES per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 130.70 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 129.91 KES per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 130.53 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 13,053 KES. The official rate says 12,991 KES.

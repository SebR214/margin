# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 129.85 KES per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 130.71 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

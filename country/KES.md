# In Kenya a dollar costs 0.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 129.84 KES per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 130.28 KES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In South Africa a dollar costs 9.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 16.58 ZAR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 18.13 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,813 ZAR. The official rate says 1,658 ZAR.

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$1,500 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

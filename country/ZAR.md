# In South Africa a dollar costs 8.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 16.58 ZAR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 17.95 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,795 ZAR. The official rate says 1,658 ZAR.

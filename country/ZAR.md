# In South Africa a dollar costs 10.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 16.4 ZAR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 18.08 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,808 ZAR. The official rate says 1,640 ZAR.

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$1,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

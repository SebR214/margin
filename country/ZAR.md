# In South Africa a dollar costs 8.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 16.64 ZAR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 17.97 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

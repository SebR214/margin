# In South Africa a dollar costs 9.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 16.5 ZAR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 18.04 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,804 ZAR. The official rate says 1,650 ZAR.

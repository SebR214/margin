# In South Africa a dollar costs 9.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 16.6 ZAR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 18.15 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,815 ZAR. The official rate says 1,660 ZAR.

# In South Africa a dollar costs 10.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 20:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 16.37 ZAR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 18.06 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,806 ZAR. The official rate says 1,637 ZAR.

# In South Africa a dollar costs 10.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 16.4 ZAR per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 18.15 ZAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,815 ZAR. The official rate says 1,640 ZAR.

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$500 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Mexico a dollar costs 0.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 18.63 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.78 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.96 MXN, 1.8% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,878 MXN. The official rate says 1,863 MXN.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

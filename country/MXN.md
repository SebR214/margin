# In Mexico a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 18.68 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.69 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.76 MXN, 0.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,869 MXN. The official rate says 1,868 MXN.

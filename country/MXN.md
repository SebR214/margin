# In Mexico a dollar costs 0.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 18.63 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.70 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.76 MXN, 0.7% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,870 MXN. The official rate says 1,863 MXN.

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Mexico a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 18.44 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.48 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.73 MXN, 1.5% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,848 MXN. The official rate says 1,844 MXN.

# In Mexico a dollar costs 0.2% less than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 18.68 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.64 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.80 MXN, 0.6% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,864 MXN. The official rate says 1,868 MXN.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is typical of the record since 10 Aug 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between −0.3% and 0.4%.

On 6 Oct this number started coming from an order book instead of person-to-person ads.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

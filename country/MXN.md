# In Mexico a dollar costs 0.2% less than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 18.73 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.70 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.76 MXN, 0.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,870 MXN. The official rate says 1,873 MXN.

# In Mexico a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 18.72 MXN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Mexico trade stablecoins, one costs 18.73 MXN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 18.88 MXN, 0.8% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 1,873 MXN. The official rate says 1,872 MXN.

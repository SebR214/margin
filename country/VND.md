# In Vietnam a dollar costs 1.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 25,831.15 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,158 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,615,825 VND. The official rate says 2,583,115 VND.

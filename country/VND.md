# In Vietnam a dollar costs 1.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 25,829.65 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,168 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

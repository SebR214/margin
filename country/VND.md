# In Vietnam a dollar costs 1.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 25,822.18 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,219 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,621,900 VND. The official rate says 2,582,218 VND.

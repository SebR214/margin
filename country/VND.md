# In Vietnam a dollar costs 2.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 25,822.18 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,384 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Vietnam a dollar costs 1.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 25,846.7 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,115 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

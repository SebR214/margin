# In Vietnam a dollar costs 1.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 25,846.7 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,095 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,609,475 VND. The official rate says 2,584,670 VND.

# In Vietnam a dollar costs 0.8% more than the official rate

The official rate is 25,846.2 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,055 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,605,475 VND. The official rate says 2,584,620 VND.

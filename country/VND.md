# In Vietnam a dollar costs 1.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 25,822.18 VND per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 26,278 VND. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

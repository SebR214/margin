# In Egypt a dollar costs 1.6% more than the official rate

The official rate is 54.49 EGP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 55.39 EGP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 5,539 EGP. The official rate says 5,449 EGP.

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

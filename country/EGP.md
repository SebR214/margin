# In Egypt a dollar costs 2.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 55.68 EGP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 57.21 EGP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 5,721 EGP. The official rate says 5,568 EGP.

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct

# In Egypt a dollar costs 1.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 54.39 EGP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 55.13 EGP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 5,513 EGP. The official rate says 5,439 EGP.

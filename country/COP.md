# In Colombia a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 3,183.99 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,183 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 318,277 COP. The official rate says 318,399 COP.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Colombia a dollar costs 0.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 3,173.54 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,185 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 318,455 COP. The official rate says 317,354 COP.

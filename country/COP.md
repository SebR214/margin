# In Colombia a dollar costs 1.0% less than the official rate

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 3,183.99 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,151 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 315,137 COP. The official rate says 318,399 COP.

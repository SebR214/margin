# In Colombia a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 20:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 3,173.54 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,173 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 317,346 COP. The official rate says 317,354 COP.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Colombia a dollar costs 1.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 3,155.2 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,187 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 318,725 COP. The official rate says 315,520 COP.

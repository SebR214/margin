# In Colombia a dollar costs 1.0% less than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 3,179.86 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,149 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 314,915 COP. The official rate says 317,986 COP.

# In Colombia a dollar costs 0.9% less than the official rate

Prices from the 20:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 3,170.49 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,143 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 314,300 COP. The official rate says 317,049 COP.

# In Colombia a dollar costs 0.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 3,153.77 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,174 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 317,420 COP. The official rate says 315,377 COP.

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

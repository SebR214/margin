# In Colombia a dollar costs 0.9% less than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 3,180.08 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,152 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 315,200 COP. The official rate says 318,008 COP.

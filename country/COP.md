# In Colombia a dollar costs 0.9% more than the official rate

The official rate is 3,153.77 COP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,183 COP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 318,348 COP. The official rate says 315,377 COP.

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

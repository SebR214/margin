# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 4.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 448.77 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 468.37 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

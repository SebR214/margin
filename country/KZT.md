# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 5.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 449.59 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 473.00 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

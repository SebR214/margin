# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 5.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 451.3 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 475.42 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

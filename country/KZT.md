# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 5.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 449.77 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 472.80 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 47,280 KZT. The official rate says 44,977 KZT.

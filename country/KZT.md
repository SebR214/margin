Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 448.59 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 469.50 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

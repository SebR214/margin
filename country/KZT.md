# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 4.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 450.27 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 471.77 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

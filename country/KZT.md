# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 6.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 452.62 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 480.50 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

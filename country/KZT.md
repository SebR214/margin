Prices from the 20:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 449.77 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 475.42 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

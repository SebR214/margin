# In Kazakhstan a dollar costs 5.1% more than the official rate

The official rate is 450.27 KZT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 473.23 KZT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 47,323 KZT. The official rate says 45,027 KZT.

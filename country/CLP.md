Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 974.77 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 988.94 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

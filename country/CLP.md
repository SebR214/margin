# In Chile a dollar costs 1.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 974.77 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 992.69 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 99,269 CLP. The official rate says 97,477 CLP.

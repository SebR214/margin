# In Chile a dollar costs 2.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 984.72 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,008 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

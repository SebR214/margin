# In Chile a dollar costs 1.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 989.53 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,006 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 100,642 CLP. The official rate says 98,953 CLP.

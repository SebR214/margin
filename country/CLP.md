# In Chile a dollar costs 1.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 981.06 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 993.47 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 99,347 CLP. The official rate says 98,106 CLP.

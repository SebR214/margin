# In Chile a dollar costs 0.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 981.25 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 985.14 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

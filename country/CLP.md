# In Chile a dollar costs 2.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 984.68 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,007 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 100,743 CLP. The official rate says 98,468 CLP.

# In Chile a dollar costs 2.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 989.53 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,011 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 101,103 CLP. The official rate says 98,953 CLP.

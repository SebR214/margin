# In Chile a dollar costs 1.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 981.06 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 993.93 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

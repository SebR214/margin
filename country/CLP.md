# In Chile a dollar costs 0.4% more than the official rate

The official rate is 981.25 CLP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 985.02 CLP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 98,502 CLP. The official rate says 98,125 CLP.

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

# In Tanzania a dollar costs 0.4% more than the official rate

The official rate is 2,641.3 TZS per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2,653 TZS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 265,299 TZS. The official rate says 264,130 TZS.

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

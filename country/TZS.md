# In Tanzania a dollar costs 1.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 2,649.57 TZS per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2,684 TZS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 268,425 TZS. The official rate says 264,957 TZS.

# In Tanzania a dollar costs 1.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 2,647.55 TZS per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2,679 TZS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 267,950 TZS. The official rate says 264,755 TZS.

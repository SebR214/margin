# In Tanzania a dollar costs 1.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 2,640.5 TZS per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2,671 TZS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 267,070 TZS. The official rate says 264,050 TZS.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

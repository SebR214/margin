# In Tanzania a dollar costs 1.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 2,644.92 TZS per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2,670 TZS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

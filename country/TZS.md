# In Tanzania a dollar costs 1.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 2,649.57 TZS per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2,681 TZS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

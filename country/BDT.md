# In Bangladesh a dollar costs 4.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 20:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 123.72 BDT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 128.66 BDT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 12,866 BDT. The official rate says 12,372 BDT.

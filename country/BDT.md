# In Bangladesh a dollar costs 3.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 123.83 BDT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 128.23 BDT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 12,823 BDT. The official rate says 12,383 BDT.

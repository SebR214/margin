# In Bangladesh a dollar costs 4.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 123.5 BDT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 128.50 BDT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 12,850 BDT. The official rate says 12,350 BDT.

# In Bangladesh a dollar costs 4.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 123.72 BDT per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 129.07 BDT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

For US$100, people pay 12,907 BDT. The official rate says 12,372 BDT.

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

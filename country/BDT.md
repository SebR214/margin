# In Bangladesh a dollar costs 3.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 123.71 BDT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 128.37 BDT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 12,837 BDT. The official rate says 12,371 BDT.

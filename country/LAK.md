# In Laos a dollar costs 4.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 22,295.92 LAK per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 23,200 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,320,000 LAK. The official rate says 2,229,592 LAK.

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$4,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

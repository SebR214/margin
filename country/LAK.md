# In Laos a dollar costs 3.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 22,358.48 LAK per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 23,142 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,314,150 LAK. The official rate says 2,235,848 LAK.

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

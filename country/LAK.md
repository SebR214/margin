# In Laos a dollar costs 2.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 22,295.92 LAK per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,792 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,279,200 LAK. The official rate says 2,229,592 LAK.

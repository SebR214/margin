# In Laos a dollar costs 3.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 22,358.48 LAK per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 23,052 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,305,225 LAK. The official rate says 2,235,848 LAK.

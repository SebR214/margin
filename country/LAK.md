# In Laos a dollar costs 2.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 22,322.01 LAK per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,791 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,279,050 LAK. The official rate says 2,232,201 LAK.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

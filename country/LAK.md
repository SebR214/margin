# In Laos a dollar costs 3.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 22,278.95 LAK per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,998 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,299,800 LAK. The official rate says 2,227,895 LAK.

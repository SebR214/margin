# In Laos a dollar costs 3.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 22,334.49 LAK per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 23,068 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,306,750 LAK. The official rate says 2,233,449 LAK.

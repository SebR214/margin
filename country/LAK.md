# In Laos a dollar costs 1.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 22,334.46 LAK per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,715 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,271,500 LAK. The official rate says 2,233,446 LAK.

# In Laos a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 22,322.01 LAK per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,770 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

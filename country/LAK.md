# In Laos a dollar costs 2.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 22,295.31 LAK per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,921 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,292,125 LAK. The official rate says 2,229,531 LAK.

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

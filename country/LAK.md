# In Laos a dollar costs 1.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 22,322.01 LAK per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 22,740 LAK. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,273,950 LAK. The official rate says 2,232,201 LAK.

# In the Philippines a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 63.53 PHP per dollar. On the exchanges where people in the Philippines trade stablecoins, one costs 63.56 PHP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 63.75 PHP, 0.4% over the official rate.

# In the Philippines a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 63.68 PHP per dollar. On the exchanges where people in the Philippines trade stablecoins, one costs 63.83 PHP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 64.23 PHP, 0.9% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 6,383 PHP. The official rate says 6,368 PHP.

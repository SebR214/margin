# In the Philippines a dollar costs 0.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 63.09 PHP per dollar. On the exchanges where people in the Philippines trade stablecoins, one costs 63.21 PHP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 63.36 PHP, 0.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 6,321 PHP. The official rate says 6,309 PHP.

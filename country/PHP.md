# In the Philippines a dollar costs 0.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 63.96 PHP per dollar. On the exchanges where people in the Philippines trade stablecoins, one costs 64.00 PHP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

The official rate is 458.56 SDG per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 10,249 SDG. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

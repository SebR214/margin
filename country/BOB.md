# In Bolivia a dollar costs 3.5% more than the official rate

The official rate is 11.69 BOB per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 12.10 BOB. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,210 BOB. The official rate says 1,169 BOB.

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

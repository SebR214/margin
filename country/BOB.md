# In Bolivia a dollar costs 3.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 11.74 BOB per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 12.17 BOB. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,217 BOB. The official rate says 1,174 BOB.

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

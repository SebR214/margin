# In Bolivia a dollar costs 2.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 11.84 BOB per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 12.13 BOB. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,213 BOB. The official rate says 1,184 BOB.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Bolivia a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 11.73 BOB per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 11.97 BOB. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,197 BOB. The official rate says 1,173 BOB.

# In Bolivia a dollar costs 2.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 11.84 BOB per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 12.18 BOB. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,218 BOB. The official rate says 1,184 BOB.

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct

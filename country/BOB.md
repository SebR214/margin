# In Bolivia a dollar costs 2.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 11.74 BOB per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 12.04 BOB. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,204 BOB. The official rate says 1,174 BOB.

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is typical of the record since 2 Sep 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between −5.9% and 4.6%.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

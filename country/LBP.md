# In Lebanon a dollar costs 1.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 89,500 LBP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 90,787 LBP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 9,078,722 LBP. The official rate says 8,950,000 LBP.

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

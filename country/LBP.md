# In Lebanon a dollar costs 2.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 89,500 LBP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 91,413 LBP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 9,141,250 LBP. The official rate says 8,950,000 LBP.

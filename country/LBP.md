# In Lebanon a dollar costs 1.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 89,500 LBP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 90,450 LBP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 9,045,000 LBP. The official rate says 8,950,000 LBP.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Lebanon a dollar costs 1.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 89,500 LBP per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 90,358 LBP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 9,035,750 LBP. The official rate says 8,950,000 LBP.

# In Lebanon a dollar costs 1.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 89,500 LBP per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 90,525 LBP. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 9,052,499 LBP. The official rate says 8,950,000 LBP.

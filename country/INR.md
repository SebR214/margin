# In India a dollar costs 6.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 95.04 INR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in India trade stablecoins, one costs 100.99 INR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance, a dollar costs 105.00 INR, 10.5% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 10,099 INR. The official rate says 9,504 INR.

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

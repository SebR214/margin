# In India a dollar costs 6.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 95 INR per dollar. On the exchanges where people in India trade stablecoins, one costs 101.25 INR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance, a dollar costs 103.71 INR, 9.2% over the official rate.

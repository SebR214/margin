# In Mongolia a dollar costs 3.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 3,583.19 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,708 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 370,756 MNT. The official rate says 358,319 MNT.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

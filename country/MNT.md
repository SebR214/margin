# In Mongolia a dollar costs 3.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 3,578.92 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,716 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 371,595 MNT. The official rate says 357,892 MNT.

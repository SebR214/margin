# In Mongolia a dollar costs 4.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 3,582.17 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,727 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 372,740 MNT. The official rate says 358,217 MNT.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is near the widest it has been since 5 Sep 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between 1.3% and 3.7%.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

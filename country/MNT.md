# In Mongolia a dollar costs 3.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 3,580.04 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,693 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 369,322 MNT. The official rate says 358,004 MNT.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

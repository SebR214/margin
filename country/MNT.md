# In Mongolia a dollar costs 2.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 3,588.63 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,678 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 367,783 MNT. The official rate says 358,863 MNT.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

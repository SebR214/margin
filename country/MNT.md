# In Mongolia a dollar costs 3.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 3,583.33 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,712 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 371,235 MNT. The official rate says 358,333 MNT.

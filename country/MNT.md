# In Mongolia a dollar costs 2.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 3,588.63 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,691 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 369,130 MNT. The official rate says 358,863 MNT.

# In Mongolia a dollar costs 2.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 3,585.4 MNT per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3,686 MNT. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 368,588 MNT. The official rate says 358,540 MNT.

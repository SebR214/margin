Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 2 Nov.

The official rate is 587.75 XOF per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 612.19 XOF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

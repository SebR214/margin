# In West African CFA a dollar costs 3.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 584.27 XOF per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 602.50 XOF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

For US$100, people pay 60,250 XOF. The official rate says 58,427 XOF.

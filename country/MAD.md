# In Morocco a dollar costs 7.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 9.81 MAD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 10.55 MAD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,055 MAD. The official rate says 981.47 MAD.

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is near the widest it has been since 5 Sep 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between 3.2% and 6.2%.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

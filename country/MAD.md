# In Morocco a dollar costs 8.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 9.83 MAD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 10.63 MAD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,063 MAD. The official rate says 982.84 MAD.

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

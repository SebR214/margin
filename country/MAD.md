# In Morocco a dollar costs 9.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 9.82 MAD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 10.78 MAD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,078 MAD. The official rate says 981.53 MAD.

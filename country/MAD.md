# In Morocco a dollar costs 7.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 9.81 MAD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 10.59 MAD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,059 MAD. The official rate says 981.29 MAD.

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

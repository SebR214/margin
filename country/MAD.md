# In Morocco a dollar costs 6.4% more than the official rate

The official rate is 9.8 MAD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 10.43 MAD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 1,043 MAD. The official rate says 979.93 MAD.

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

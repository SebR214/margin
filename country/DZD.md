# In Algeria a dollar costs 91.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 134.55 DZD per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 257.20 DZD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

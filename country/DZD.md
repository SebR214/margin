# In Algeria a dollar costs 90.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 134.64 DZD per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 257.00 DZD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Algeria a dollar costs 92.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 19:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 134.55 DZD per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 258.70 DZD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 25,870 DZD. The official rate says 13,455 DZD.

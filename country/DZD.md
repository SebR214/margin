# In Algeria a dollar costs 91.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 134.64 DZD per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 257.92 DZD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 25,792 DZD. The official rate says 13,464 DZD.

# In Georgia a dollar costs 0.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 2.6 GEL per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2.61 GEL. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

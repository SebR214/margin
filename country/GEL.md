# In Georgia a dollar costs 1.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 2.6 GEL per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 2.62 GEL. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 262.20 GEL. The official rate says 259.67 GEL.

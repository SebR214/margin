# In Ukraine a dollar costs 4.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 45.27 UAH per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 47.36 UAH. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

For US$100, people pay 4,736 UAH. The official rate says 4,527 UAH.

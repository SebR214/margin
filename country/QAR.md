# In Qatar a dollar costs 3.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 3.64 QAR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3.78 QAR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 377.85 QAR. The official rate says 364.00 QAR.

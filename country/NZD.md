# In New Zealand a dollar costs 0.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 1.76 NZD per dollar. On the exchanges where people in New Zealand trade stablecoins, one costs 1.77 NZD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 176.67 NZD. The official rate says 176.04 NZD.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

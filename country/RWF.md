# In Rwanda a dollar costs 1.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 1,477.71 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,497 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 149,699 RWF. The official rate says 147,771 RWF.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

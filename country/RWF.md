# In Rwanda a dollar costs 1.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 1,478.59 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,495 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 149,497 RWF. The official rate says 147,859 RWF.

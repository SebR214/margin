# In Rwanda a dollar costs 1.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 1,478.26 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,494 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 149,427 RWF. The official rate says 147,826 RWF.

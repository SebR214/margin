# In Rwanda a dollar costs 1.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 1,477.88 RWF per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,503 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 150,332 RWF. The official rate says 147,788 RWF.

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

# In Rwanda a dollar costs 0.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 05:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 1,477.71 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,490 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 148,998 RWF. The official rate says 147,771 RWF.

# In Rwanda a dollar costs 1.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 1,475.49 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,496 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 149,605 RWF. The official rate says 147,549 RWF.

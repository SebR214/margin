# In Rwanda a dollar costs 0.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 1,477.88 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,491 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 149,125 RWF. The official rate says 147,788 RWF.

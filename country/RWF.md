# In Rwanda a dollar costs 0.5% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 1,477.84 RWF per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,485 RWF. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 148,534 RWF. The official rate says 147,784 RWF.

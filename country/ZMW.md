# In Zambia a dollar costs 3.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 20.15 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.79 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,079 ZMW. The official rate says 2,015 ZMW.

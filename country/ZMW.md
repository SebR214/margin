# In Zambia a dollar costs 4.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 20.15 ZMW per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 21.14 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,114 ZMW. The official rate says 2,015 ZMW.

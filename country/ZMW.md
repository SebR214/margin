Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 20.14 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.63 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Zambia a dollar costs 3.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 20.14 ZMW per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.91 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Sellers on Binance and OKX agree on this price. Buyers there are offering more than sellers ask this hour, which is unusual.

For US$100, people pay 2,091 ZMW. The official rate says 2,014 ZMW.

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

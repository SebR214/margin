# In Zambia a dollar costs 2.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 12:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 20.09 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.65 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,065 ZMW. The official rate says 2,009 ZMW.

# In Zambia a dollar costs 2.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 20.15 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.63 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Zambia a dollar costs 4.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 20.27 ZMW per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 21.13 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,113 ZMW. The official rate says 2,027 ZMW.

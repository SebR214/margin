# In Zambia a dollar costs 1.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 20.27 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.64 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,064 ZMW. The official rate says 2,027 ZMW.

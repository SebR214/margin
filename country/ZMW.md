# In Zambia a dollar costs 2.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 20.09 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.62 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

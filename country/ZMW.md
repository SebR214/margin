# In Zambia a dollar costs 3.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 20.14 ZMW per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.78 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Zambia a dollar costs 1.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 10:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 20.15 ZMW per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 20.50 ZMW. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 2,050 ZMW. The official rate says 2,015 ZMW.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

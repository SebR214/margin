# In Nigeria a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 1,359.91 NGN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Nigeria trade stablecoins, one costs 1,387 NGN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on OKX, a dollar costs 1,425 NGN, 4.7% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 138,696 NGN. The official rate says 135,991 NGN.

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

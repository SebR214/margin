# In Nigeria a dollar costs 2.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 1,332.68 NGN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Nigeria trade stablecoins, one costs 1,361 NGN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on OKX, a dollar costs 1,366 NGN, 2.5% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 136,100 NGN. The official rate says 133,268 NGN.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is near the narrowest it has been since 9 Oct 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between 2.1% and 2.4%.

On 9 Oct this number started coming from an order book instead of an independent price source.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

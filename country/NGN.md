# In Nigeria a dollar costs 4.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 22:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 1,329.8 NGN per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Nigeria trade stablecoins, one costs 1,384 NGN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on OKX, a dollar costs 1,391 NGN, 4.6% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 138,433 NGN. The official rate says 132,980 NGN.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from an order book. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

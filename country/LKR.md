# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 2.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 07:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 332.17 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 341.93 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 34,193 LKR. The official rate says 33,217 LKR.

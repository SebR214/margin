# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 3.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 00:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 331.71 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 342.45 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 3.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 332.04 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 341.85 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 34,185 LKR. The official rate says 33,204 LKR.

# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 2.7% more than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 332.01 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 341.00 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 34,100 LKR. The official rate says 33,201 LKR.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

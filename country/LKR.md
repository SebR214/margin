# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 3.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 03:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 332.01 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 342.20 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 34,220 LKR. The official rate says 33,201 LKR.

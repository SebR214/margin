# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 2.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 331.71 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 340.32 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 34,032 LKR. The official rate says 33,171 LKR.

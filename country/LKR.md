# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 2.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 16:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 331.6 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 339.63 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

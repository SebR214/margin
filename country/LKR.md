# In Sri Lanka a dollar costs 2.0% more than the official rate

The official rate is 331.6 LKR per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 338.07 LKR. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 33,807 LKR. The official rate says 33,160 LKR.

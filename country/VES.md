# In Venezuela a dollar costs 17.3% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 886.23 VES per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Venezuela trade stablecoins, one costs 1,040 VES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,005 VES, 13.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 103,967 VES. The official rate says 88,623 VES.

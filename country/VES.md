# In Venezuela a dollar costs 18.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 883.4 VES per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Venezuela trade stablecoins, one costs 1,046 VES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,009 VES, 14.2% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 104,618 VES. The official rate says 88,340 VES.

# In Venezuela a dollar costs 16.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 13:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 886.23 VES per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Venezuela trade stablecoins, one costs 1,036 VES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,004 VES, 13.3% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 103,622 VES. The official rate says 88,623 VES.

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

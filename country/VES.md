# In Venezuela a dollar costs 18.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 884.75 VES per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Venezuela trade stablecoins, one costs 1,045 VES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,007 VES, 13.8% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 104,504 VES. The official rate says 88,475 VES.

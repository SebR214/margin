# In Venezuela a dollar costs 16.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 18:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 887.3 VES per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Venezuela trade stablecoins, one costs 1,036 VES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,008 VES, 13.6% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 103,642 VES. The official rate says 88,730 VES.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

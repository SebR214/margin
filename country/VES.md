# In Venezuela a dollar costs 16.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 887.3 VES per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Venezuela trade stablecoins, one costs 1,030 VES. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,042 VES, 17.5% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 103,002 VES. The official rate says 88,730 VES.

# In Argentina a dollar costs 9.9% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 1,529.5 ARS per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Argentina trade stablecoins, one costs 1,681 ARS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,667 ARS, 9.0% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 168,050 ARS. The official rate says 152,950 ARS.

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

# In Argentina a dollar costs 8.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 1,529.5 ARS per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Argentina trade stablecoins, one costs 1,655 ARS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,647 ARS, 7.7% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 165,500 ARS. The official rate says 152,950 ARS.

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is near the widest it has been since 2 Sep 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between 6.1% and 7.7%.

On 6 Oct this number started coming from broker quotes instead of person-to-person ads.

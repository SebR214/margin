# In Argentina a dollar costs 7.6% more than the official rate

The official rate is 1,525 ARS per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Argentina trade stablecoins, one costs 1,641 ARS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,633 ARS, 7.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 164,110 ARS. The official rate says 152,500 ARS.

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

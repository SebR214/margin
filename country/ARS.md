# In Argentina a dollar costs 8.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 1,529.5 ARS per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Argentina trade stablecoins, one costs 1,659 ARS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,654 ARS, 8.1% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 165,856 ARS. The official rate says 152,950 ARS.

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$4,500 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

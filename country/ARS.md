# In Argentina a dollar costs 8.0% more than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 1,528 ARS per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Argentina trade stablecoins, one costs 1,651 ARS. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 1,657 ARS, 8.4% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 165,071 ARS. The official rate says 152,800 ARS.

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, at least US$5,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

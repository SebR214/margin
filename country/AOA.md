# In Angola a dollar costs 30.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 921.96 AOA per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,204 AOA. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 120,449 AOA. The official rate says 92,196 AOA.

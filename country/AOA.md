# In Angola a dollar costs 30.8% more than the official rate

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 27 Oct.

The official rate is 926.9 AOA per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,212 AOA. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 121,199 AOA. The official rate says 92,690 AOA.

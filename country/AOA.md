# In Angola a dollar costs 28.4% more than the official rate

Prices from the 17:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 930.69 AOA per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 1,195 AOA. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 119,500 AOA. The official rate says 93,069 AOA.

# In Peru a dollar costs 1.2% more than the official rate

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 3.43 PEN per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3.47 PEN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 347.35 PEN. The official rate says 343.30 PEN.

Prices from the 09:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$4,000 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

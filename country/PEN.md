# In Peru a dollar costs 2.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 04:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 3.44 PEN per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3.52 PEN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 351.55 PEN. The official rate says 344.16 PEN.

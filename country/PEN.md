# In Peru a dollar costs 1.6% more than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 3.44 PEN per dollar. On Binance, in a marketplace where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 3.50 PEN. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

For US$100, people pay 349.75 PEN. The official rate says 344.09 PEN.

This price is set from person-to-person ads. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.1% less than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 49.73 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.69 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.30 TRY, 5.2% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,969 TRY. The official rate says 4,973 TRY.

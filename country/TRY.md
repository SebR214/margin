# In Turkey a dollar costs what the official rate says

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct.

The official rate is 49.73 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.71 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.81 TRY, 6.2% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,971 TRY. The official rate says 4,973 TRY.

Prices from the 21:00 UTC hour, 24 Oct

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from order books. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

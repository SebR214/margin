# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.3% less than the official rate

Prices from the 14:00 UTC hour, 26 Oct.

The official rate is 49.79 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.65 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.19 TRY, 4.8% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,965 TRY. The official rate says 4,979 TRY.

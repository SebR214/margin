# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.1% less than the official rate

Prices from the 06:00 UTC hour, 25 Oct.

The official rate is 49.74 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.68 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 53.09 TRY, 6.7% over the official rate.

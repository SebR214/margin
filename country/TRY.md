# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.2% less than the official rate

Prices from the 15:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 49.64 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.54 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.10 TRY, 4.9% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,954 TRY. The official rate says 4,964 TRY.

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

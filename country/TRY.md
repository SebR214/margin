# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.3% less than the official rate

Prices from the 23:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 49.6 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.44 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.50 TRY, 5.8% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,944 TRY. The official rate says 4,960 TRY.

## How the difference moved, day by day

One point per day, the price we published that day. 0% is the official rate.

Today's difference is typical of the record since 10 Aug 2026, counting only our own readings. On most days it sits between −0.3% and 0.0%.

On 6 Oct this number started coming from order books instead of person-to-person ads.

## Where this price comes from

This price is set from order books. A country's price comes from exchange order books first, then broker quotes, then offers between people on Binance and OKX.

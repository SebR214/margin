# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.3% less than the official rate

Prices from the 11:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct.

The official rate is 49.6 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.46 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 51.93 TRY, 4.7% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,946 TRY. The official rate says 4,960 TRY.

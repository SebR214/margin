# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.4% less than the official rate

Prices from the 01:00 UTC hour, 22 Oct.

The official rate is 49.64 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.44 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.85 TRY, 6.5% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,944 TRY. The official rate says 4,964 TRY.

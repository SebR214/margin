# In Turkey a dollar costs 0.4% less than the official rate

The official rate is 49.6 TRY per dollar. On the exchanges where people in Turkey trade stablecoins, one costs 49.39 TRY. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

Between people on Binance and OKX, a dollar costs 52.02 TRY, 4.9% over the official rate.

For US$100, people pay 4,939 TRY. The official rate says 4,960 TRY.

Prices from the 08:00 UTC hour, 21 Oct

Taking the cheapest offers first, only US$3,500 can be bought before a dollar here costs 1% more.

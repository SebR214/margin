# In Jordan a dollar costs 4.1% more than the official rate

Prices from the 02:00 UTC hour, 23 Oct.

The official rate is 0.71 JOD per dollar. On Binance and OKX, marketplaces where people sell stablecoins to each other, one costs 0.74 JOD. A stablecoin is a coin that stays worth one US dollar.

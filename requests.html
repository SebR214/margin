Ask for a market we don't have yet.

A commission is a request for a currency or a corridor this project does not collect. Filing one opens a public issue: a candidate source is checked against an independent reference the same way every number on this site is checked, and either the market goes live saying "collecting since <date>", or the attempt is shown here, rejected, with exactly what was checked and why. Nothing is accepted without that check, and nothing is rejected without saying so.

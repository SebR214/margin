Where the cost goes

Three things happen to your money: you buy the coin, you move it, you sell it. Each bar is what that step costs at this amount. Bars left of the line are money you gain, right of the line are money you lose. The dashed line is the cheapest ordinary provider, same second.

It is worst at the amounts people actually send

The blockchain charges the same flat fee whatever you send, so it barely registers on a large transfer and dominates a small one. S$200 is highlighted.

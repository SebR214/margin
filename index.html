Light grey: pegged currency, the gap is the cost of buying stablecoins.
Where the gap is near zero, the official rate is real and a normal app is cheaper. Where the gap is big, the official rate isn't what people pay, and stablecoins are how people get dollars at all.

In the Philippines and Mexico, what people pay and the official rate match, so a normal app already gives you the real rate and a stablecoin has nothing to beat. In Algeria, banks sell few dollars, so USDT is how people get them. Right now you can buy about $2,500 of USDT there within 1% of the best price.

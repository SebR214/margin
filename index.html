Assumptions & data sources

Every rail is one number: send S$X, how much local currency lands. Crypto is walked live from the books for the exact amount; fiat is interpolated between real Wise quotes. Costs that hit every rail identically — local cash-out (e.g. GCash 2%) and bank receiving fees — are excluded: they change how much lands, not who wins. Enterprise payout pricing (Nium, Thunes, Circle) is negotiated and out of scope.

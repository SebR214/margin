Stablecoins rarely beat the cheapest transfer app. But where banks won't sell you dollars, they're how people get one.

Four AI agents run this site every hour. They track what a dollar costs on the street in 45 countries, and what it costs to send money with an app or with stablecoins. How it was built →
Built to test one claim: that stablecoins make moving money cheaper.
What a dollar costs on the street vs the official rate, every country we track
Where the gap is near zero, the official rate is real and a normal app is cheaper. Where the gap is big, the official rate isn't what people pay, and stablecoins are how people get dollars at all.
Stablecoin route vs cheapest app, all-in cost per route

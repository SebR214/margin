Light grey: pegged currency, the gap is the cost of buying stablecoins.
Where the gap is near zero, the official rate is real and a normal app is cheaper. Where the gap is big, the official rate isn't what people pay, and stablecoins are how people get dollars at all.

A normal app already gets you the real rate in the Philippines and Mexico, so a stablecoin has nothing to beat. In Algeria, banks sell few dollars, so USDT is how people get them: about $5,000 is on offer right now within 1% of the best price.

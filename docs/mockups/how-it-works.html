We measure two things every hour: what a dollar really costs, and what sending money costs

What a dollar costs: the price of USDT, divided by the official rate

Every hour we take the middle price of the 10 cheapest ads selling USDT in each country, on Binance P2P or a local crypto exchange, and divide it by the official rate. The number you see is the median of the last 24 hours. A country with fewer than 10 sellers gets no price, and we say why.

What sending money costs: what arrives, compared with the real exchange rate

For each provider we compare what the recipient gets with what they would get at the mid-market rate, at five amounts. Airwallex, Instarem, Wise and WorldRemit quote us directly. The rest come from a public price comparison. The stablecoin route is priced on real exchange order books, with every fee included.

What we don't measure yet

  • The official rate only updates once a day, so it can be up to 24 hours behind the market.
  • Bank fees for getting money onto and off the exchanges.
  • Prices for repeat senders. Some app prices are first-transfer offers.
  • Cash dollars at money changers.

The week of 31 August 2026 to 6 September 2026.

One figure per country: the median of every hour that country cleared this week, so a single dislocated hour cannot carry the week. This page is frozen -- once written it is never recomputed, even if the source files change later.

Every figure is read from data/weekly/2026-W36.json, written once when the week ended and never rewritten.
